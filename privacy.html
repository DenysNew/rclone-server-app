Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

rclone-server is a personal tool: a private rclone instance used only by its owner.

  • Who uses it. The application is used exclusively by its owner. It is not available to anyone else.
  • What it accesses. It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, to copy the owner's files to and from the owner's own server.
  • Sharing. No data is shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party.
  • Storage. Data and access tokens are not stored anywhere other than the owner's own server.
  • Google user data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
